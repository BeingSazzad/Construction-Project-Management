# Construction Project Management Skill

## 1. Domain Terminology Standards

- **Active Projects (Not "Active Sites"):** Refers to full construction contracts/projects (`Riverside Office Complex`, `Downtown Commercial Tower`).
- **Site Operations / Field Telemetry:** Refers to physical field locations, CCTV cameras, weather, and labor headcount.
- **Company Owner & Principal (Not SaaS App Admin):** Refers to the construction firm principal (`Alex Chen`, Avery & Marsh Construction) who purchased the software subscription.

## 2. Core Operational Modules

1. **Portfolio Workspace:** Overview, Progress, Gantt Timeline, Cost Codes, Documents, Daily Logs, Blueprints.
2. **Deals CRM Pipeline:** Lead, Estimating, Proposal, Won Deals.
3. **Budgets Hub:** CSI MasterFormat (Division 03 Concrete, Division 05 Metals, Division 26 Electrical).
4. **Financing & Capital:** Bank Draws, Lien Waivers, Retainage.
